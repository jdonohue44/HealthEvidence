About

Health Evidence cuts through the noise. We highlight research that's actually reliable, and call out popular claims that aren't.

Our goal is simple: help you know what you can trust.

How We Decide

Strong Evidence comes from well-designed experiments where people were randomly assigned to groups, with clear results and enough participants to be reliable.

Weak Evidence often comes from studies that only observed patterns without testing them, making it impossible to know if one thing actually caused another.

Data Sources

Gold standard studies are sourced from ClinicalTrials.gov and the WHO ICTRP Trial Search portal.

Weak evidence entries are curated from peer-reviewed literature (PubMed), arXiv preprints, Data Colada, and Blind Spots.
