About

Most people are not trained experts in assessing causal research. Our goal with this website is to help you know what you can trust so you can make informed decisions about your health.

How We Decide

Strong Evidence comes from randomized experiments with lots of participants and large effects.

Weak Evidence comes from studies that only observed patterns without testing interventions, or from studies that had small effects or few participants.

Data Sources

Studies are sourced from PubMed, a database of more than 40 million citations for biomedical literature created by the National Library of Medicine.
